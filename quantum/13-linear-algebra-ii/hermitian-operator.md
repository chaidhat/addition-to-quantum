# Hermitian Operator

An [[operator|operator]] is [[hermitian-matrix|Hermitian]] if and only if the [[adjoint]] of the operator is
equal to itself.

Properties

- all [[eigenvectoreigenvalue|eigenvalues]] of a Hermitian operator are [[standard-number-systems|real]]

- All [[eigenvectoreigenvalue|eigenvectors]] of a Hermitian operator form an [[orthonormality|orthonormal]] [[basis-state|basis]]

- [[observable]] are usually some kind of Hermitian operator

The [[eigenvectoreigenvalue|eigenvalues]] represent possible measurement outcomes. [[orthonormality|Orthonormal]]
[[eigenstate]] $\left\lvert \alpha \right\rangle$ mean that these are [[definite]] value [[ket-state|states]]
