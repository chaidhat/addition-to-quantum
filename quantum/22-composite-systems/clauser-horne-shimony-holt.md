# Clauser, Horne, Shimony, and Holt (1969)
Given 

$$
A_i,B_j\in\{\pm 1\}
$$

then CHSH postulated it to be

$$
\big|\mathbb{E}[A_1 B_1] - \mathbb{E}[A_1 B_2] + \mathbb{E}[A_2 B_1] + \mathbb{E}[A_2 B_2]\big| \leq 2
$$

This is derived, explained and disproved in [[bells-theorem]]
QM actually reaches $2\sqrt{2}$  as an upperbound.
