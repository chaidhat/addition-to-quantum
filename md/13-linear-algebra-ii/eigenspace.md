# Eigenspace

An eigenspace of operator $\hat{A}$ is the [[set]] of all [[eigenvectoreigenvalue|eigenvectors]]/[[eigenstate]] with the same [[eigenvectoreigenvalue|eigenvalue]] plus the zero [[vector|vector]].

Example

If [[operator|operator]] $\hat{A}$ has [[eigenvectoreigenvalue|eigenvalue]] $\lambda$, then the $\lambda$-eigenspace of the operator $\hat{A}$ is

$$
\{\left\lvert \psi \right\rangle:\hat{A}\left\lvert \psi \right\rangle=\lambda\left\lvert \psi \right\rangle\}
$$
