# Wave Packet

Describe particles with well defined position and momentum

Take the [[wave-function]] $\psi(x)$
A wave packet is a real envelope $\phi(x)$ modulating a plane wave
$$
\psi(x) = \phi(x)\, e^{ikx}, \quad \phi(x) \in \mathbb{R}
$$
$$
\psi^*(x) = \phi(x)e^{-ikx}
$$
The envelope represents the [[probability-theory|probability]] that the particle will appear at exactly $x$. $|\psi(x)|^2=\phi^2(x)$ is the probability density for the position.
$$
P(a \le x \le b) = \int_a^b |\psi(x)|^2\,dx
$$
