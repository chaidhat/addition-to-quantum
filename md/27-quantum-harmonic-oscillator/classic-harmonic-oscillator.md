# Classic Harmonic Oscillator

Let's say something is an oscillator.

We have this restoring force

$$
E = \frac{p^2}{2m} + \tfrac{1}{2}m\omega^2 x^2
$$

where

$$
k\triangleq m\omega^2
$$

This is similar to a oscillator.

$$
F = -kx
$$

Any smooth potential with a minimum at $x_0$ expands as (via [[taylor-series]])

$$
V(x) = V(x_0) + (x-x_0)V'(x_0) + \tfrac{1}{2}(x-x_0)^2 V''(x_0) + \cdots
$$

$$
= \text{constant} + \tfrac{1}{2}k(x - x_0)^2
$$

where

$$
k\triangleq V''(x_0)
$$

### Applications
1. vibrations in materials and molecules
2. trapped atoms and ions (quantum information processing)
3. light/photons
4. circuit oscillator (LC)
