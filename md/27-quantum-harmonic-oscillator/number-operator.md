The number operator is defined as

$$
\boxed{\hat{N}=\hat{a}^\dagger\hat{a}}
$$

where $\hat{a}$ is the lowering/annihilation operator and $\hat{a}^\dagger$ is the raising/creation operator
Note the commutations

$$
[\hat{H}, \hat{a}] = \hbar\omega\,[\hat{a}^\dagger\hat{a}, \hat{a}] = \hbar\omega\,[\hat{a}^\dagger, \hat{a}]\,\hat{a} = -\hbar\omega\,\hat{a}
$$

and

$$
[\hat{H}, \hat{a}^\dagger] = +\hbar\omega\,\hat{a}^\dagger
$$
