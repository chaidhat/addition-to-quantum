A Gaussian distribution is one way of distributing [[probability-theory|random numbers]].
This comes up a lot in everyday stuff. This is because the central limit theorem says the sums of many independent random variables converge to a Gaussian.

A Gaussian is any function of the form

$$
f(x) = A\, e^{-(x-x_0)^2 / 2\sigma^2}
$$

The probability distribution normalizes it to $1$

$$
P(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}}\,e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}
$$

Properties:
1. Symmetric about $\mu$
2. Note

$$
\mathbb{E}[x]=\mu\quad \mathbb{E}[(x-\mu)^2]=\sigma^2
$$
