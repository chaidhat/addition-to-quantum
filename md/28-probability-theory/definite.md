# Definite
1. No uncertainty 
2. No [[spread]](i.e., zero [[variance]])
3. No probability distribution

An [[observables|observable]] $\hat{A}$ has a definite value $a$ on a [[ket-states|state]] $\ket{\psi}$ when
$$
\hat{A}\ket{\psi}=a\ket{\psi}
$$
i.e., $\ket{\psi}$ is an [[eigenstates|eigenstate]] of $\hat{A}$ with eigenvalue $a$
