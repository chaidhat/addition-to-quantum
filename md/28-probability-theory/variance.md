# Variance

Let $\mu=\mathbb{E}[X]$ where $\mathbb{E}[X]$ is the [[expected-value-of-observables|expected value]] of [[probability-theory|probability]] distribution $X$.

$$
\begin{gather*}
    Var(X)=\left\langle (x-\mu)^2 \right\rangle=\left\langle X^2 \right\rangle-2\mu\left\langle X \right\rangle+\mu^2\\
    =\left\langle X^2 \right\rangle-\left\langle X \right\rangle^2
\end{gather*}
$$
