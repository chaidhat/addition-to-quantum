A gate for a [[quantum-computer]]

It is like a beam splitter.
Let $H$ be an [[operator]] where

$$
H = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} = Z\,e^{i\frac{\pi}{4}Y}
$$

It creates [[superposition]] where $\ket{\pm}$ is defined in [[spin-12-systems]]

$$
H\ket{0} = \ket{+}, \qquad H\ket{1} = \ket{-}
$$

Fact about on the basis change identities:

$$
HZH = X, \qquad HXH = Z
$$
