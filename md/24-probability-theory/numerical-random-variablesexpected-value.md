# Numerical Random Variable/Expected Value

Uncertainty of a certain measurement

- a numerical RV X labels events by values that are [[standard-number-systems|real]] numbers X.

- the mean or [[expected-value-of-observables|expected value]]

$$
\begin{gather*}
    \mathbb{E}[X]=\left\langle X \right\rangle=\sum_X X\cdot P(x)
\end{gather*}
$$
It is the [[probability-theory|probability]] of x occurring multiplied by the value of x.
